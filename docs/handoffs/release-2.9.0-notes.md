Hydra Deluxe 2.9.0 adds a solo box to the Preview, like the one Clone Hero shows during a solo. The Preview's step buttons now move 10 ms, and its Star Power meter shows on every song.

**Your saved results carry over.** Nothing in this release changes how a chart is analyzed or scored, so every saved result stays as it was, and nothing reads `Stale`.

**The Preview shows a solo box.** Once you hit a solo's first note, a box appears right of the highway, against its edge, a little below the middle. It shows how much of the solo you have hit so far, like `85%`, and under that the notes, like `102 / 120 notes`. The percent is worked out the way Clone Hero works it out, so it can read one below the exact share. When you hit the solo's last note, the box shows `Perfect Solo!` for 1.5 seconds, then the solo bonus of 100 points per note, like `+12,000`, for 1.5 seconds, and then goes away. The path hits every note, so a solo always ends in `Perfect Solo!`. If a new solo starts while the box still shows the last one's result, the new solo takes over. The box works before a song is analyzed too.

**The step buttons move 10 ms.** `< 10 ms` and `10 ms >`, and the `,` (comma) and `.` (period) keys, move the playhead 10 ms. They used to read `< 5 Ticks` and `5 Ticks >` and move 5 ticks. A tick is the chart's own smallest step of time, so 5 ticks lasted longer or shorter with the tempo. A step still pauses playback.

**The Star Power meter shows on every song.** The SP meter at the right edge of the Preview, and the drain box beside it, used to disappear on a chart with no Star Power phrases. They now show on every song. On such a chart the meter stays empty and reads `0.0/4` at SP cap 4, and the drain box reads `SP drain (if activated)`.
