Hydra Deluxe 2.14.0 shows how many 2x kicks a chart has.

**Your saved results carry over.** Nothing reads `Stale` after the update, nothing is analyzed again, and no scan starts. Scores and paths don't change.

**Update from the app.** If you run 2.12.0 or later, the bar at the top of the window offers this release the next time Hydra Deluxe starts; click `Update`. If you run 2.11.0 or 2.11.1 with its update check on, a line under the toolbar offers it once its daily check runs. From 2.10.2 or earlier, download the `-setup.exe` file below and run it.

**The song header shows the 2x kick count.** 2x kicks are the extra kick notes that Clone Hero plays only with its Double Kick modifier, which the `2x Bass` setting matches. When the open chart has any, the line under the score adds how many there are and what share of the chart's kick notes they make up, in gold, like `Optimal path · 7 stars · 37 2x kicks (8%)`. It shows on every tab, whether `2x Bass` is on or off. A chart with no 2x kicks shows nothing extra.

**The Dynamics tab says whether 2x kicks count.** The `Chart` box repeats the 2x kick line, like `2x kicks: 37 of 455 kick notes (8%)`, in gold. Under it, `counted (2x Bass on)` shows in grey, or `not counted (2x Bass off)` in orange. With `2x Bass` off, the `2x kick` row in the `Kicks` table reads `2x kick (not counted)`, its dot turns grey, and the line above the table ends with `not counted (2x Bass off)`.

**Kick rows fade only when they don't count.** The `Kick` and `All kicks` rows used to look faded whenever they had no ghost or accent notes, as if they didn't count. Now a row in the `Kicks` table fades only when its notes don't count: the `2x kick` row with `2x Bass` off. The `Pads` table works as before.
