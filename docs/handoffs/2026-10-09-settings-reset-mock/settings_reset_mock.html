Analysis settings: where the reset button goes

Each tab is a clickable mock of the settings panel as it looks today, with a reset button added in a different place. The controls work. Change anything and the button label above the panel updates, just like in the app. Hover the reset button to see its tooltip, then click it. The colours, wording and defaults come from the code (theme.h, settings_bar.cpp, library_dialogs.cpp, config.h). Nothing here talks to the real app.

Same in every option. The button puts back the eight settings in this panel and nothing else. Your folders, preview volume and hit window stay as they are. It goes grey when the settings already read "defaults", and while a batch has them locked. There's no confirm box. The tooltip says what the click will undo, in the same words as the label above the panel.

The defaults are the app's built-in ones (config.h): Expert, Pro Drums on, 2x Bass on, Note Shuffle off, SP cap 4 bars, 1.0 fills off, Score range 4 scores, Path limit on at 10 ms.
