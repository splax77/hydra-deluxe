Settings bar: three ways to clear it

Each tab is a clickable mock of Hydra's top area. The controls work: change a setting and every summary updates. "Now" is today's bar, for comparison. The colours, fonts, help text and default settings come from the code (theme.h, theme.cpp, settings_bar.cpp, config.h). The library counts and song rows come from your screenshot. Nothing here is wired to the real app.
