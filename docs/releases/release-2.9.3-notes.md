Hydra Deluxe 2.9.3 changes which folds on the Paths tab start open.

**Your saved results carry over.** Nothing reads `Stale` after the update, and nothing is analyzed again.

**An activation row opens with its `Backend timings` table showing.** When you click an activation row, its `Backend timings` fold is already open. Click the fold to close it. It stays closed until you pick another path. The activation rows themselves still start closed.

**`Multiplier squeeze` and `Score breakdown` start open again.** In 2.9.2 these two folds under the activation rows started closed. They start open now. If you close one, it stays closed for every path and song you open after that, until you open it again or close Hydra Deluxe.
