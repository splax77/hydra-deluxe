Hydra Deluxe 3.0.2 fixes the words on the Paths tab's `Dynamic Hit Window:` row. In 3.0.1 the row's `frontend` and `backend` were switched on some squeezes. Your saved results carry over as they are from 3.0.0 or 3.0.1: nothing reads `Stale` and nothing is analyzed again. If you update from 2.14.0 or earlier, the 3.0.0 notes apply: your results read `Stale` once.

**Update from the app.** If you run 2.12.0 or later, the bar at the top of the window offers this release the next time Hydra Deluxe starts; click `Update`. If you run 2.11.0 or 2.11.1 with its update check on, a line under the toolbar offers it once its daily check runs. From 2.10.2 or earlier, download the `-setup.exe` file below and run it.

## Paths tab

**On a squeeze, `frontend` is the activation note and `backend` is the note at the Star Power end.** These are the same frontend and backend as the rest of Hydra Deluxe, where the `Backend timings` table lists the notes near the Star Power end. The frontend note is the one whose timing moves the Star Power end: the activation note, or the note that filled the meter when the Star Power cap cut the end short. The backend note is the squeezed note at the Star Power end, the one you squeeze in or out. The frontend note always comes first, on a squeeze in and a squeeze out alike. The words no longer say whether you hit a note early or late; the sentence above the row still does.

In 3.0.1 the row named each note by the way you hit it, so on a squeeze in, where you hit the squeezed note early, it called that note `frontend`. On Epidermis's optimal path in the test charts, activation 2's `squeeze in 300.0 ms` now reads `frontend 48.1 ms [K Y  ]   backend 67.8 ms [ R   ]`: Kick and Yellow is the activation chord and Red is the squeezed note. A squeeze out such as Burnout's activation 1 reads the same as in 3.0.1, `frontend 84.5 ms [K   G]   backend 84.3 ms [  Y  ]`.

**An early fill's row shows its note with no word.** It holds one note, the one that makes Star Power ready, which is neither the activation note nor at the Star Power end. On Tapped Out's optimal path, activation 4's early fill row reads `85.7 ms [K  B ]` with `85.7 ms safe`.

The tag's figures, the squeeze sentences, the `Early fill:` line, badges, `Rating` words and the path report's tiers are unchanged.
