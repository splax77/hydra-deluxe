Hydra Deluxe 3.4.0 adds an `Effective figures` checkbox to the Paths tab. It hides every effective figure in the Activations list until you turn it on, and then shows them in gray. A squeeze box can now also show the frontend note's effective window, on a new `Effective:` line. Your saved results carry over as they are, so nothing reads `Stale` and no scan starts.

**Update from the app.** If you run 2.12.0 or later, the bar at the top of the window offers this release the next time Hydra Deluxe starts; click `Update`. If you run 2.11.0 or 2.11.1 with its update check on, a line under the toolbar offers it once its daily check runs. From 2.10.2 or earlier, download the `-setup.exe` file below and run it.

**One checkbox shows or hides the effective figures.** The `Effective figures` checkbox sits just left of `Expand all` and `Collapse all`. It covers the `Effective:` line, the `Frontend timing scales` lines, the `(eff. ...)` figure in a squeeze sentence or a `Rating`, and the note that appears when you hover a `Timing` cell in `Backend timings`. It is off each time Hydra Deluxe starts, and the choice isn't kept between runs. While Hydra Deluxe runs, it keeps your choice from path to path and from song to song. With it on, those figures show in gray. The scale lines are no longer orange.

**A rating still follows its effective figure.** When a row has an effective figure, its `Rating` reads the band for that figure whether or not the checkbox shows the `(eff. ...)` part. On Burnout's optimal path, activation 1's squeezed-out note reads `Insane+ <-- squeezed out (-260)` with the checkbox off, and `Insane+ (eff. 163.5 ms) <-- squeezed out (-260)` with it on.

**The `Effective:` line.** When a squeeze's frontend timing scale isn't x1.00 for the way you hit its frontend note, and `Effective figures` is on, the squeeze box adds a gray line under its `Dynamic Hit Window:` row. It reads `Effective: frontend` and a figure in ms: the frontend note's window side times that scale. That is how far a hit at the edge of the window moves the Star Power end.

**A squeeze at exactly 0.0 ms reads one side.** A squeeze in at exactly 0.0 ms now uses the late side of its frontend timing scale, the same side its `safe` or `short` tag uses. Before, the scale line and the rating could read the early side while the tag read the late side, so the box and its tag could disagree.
