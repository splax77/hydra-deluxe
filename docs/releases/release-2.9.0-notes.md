Hydra Deluxe 2.9.0 adds a solo box to the Preview, like the one Clone Hero shows during a solo. The Preview's step buttons now move 10 ms, and its Star Power meter shows on every song.

**Your saved results carry over.** Nothing in this release changes how a chart is analyzed or scored, so every saved result stays as it was, and nothing reads `Stale`.

**The Preview shows a solo box.** Once you hit a solo's first note, a box appears just right of the highway, a little below the middle. It shows how much of the solo you have hit so far, like `85%`, and under that the notes, like `102 / 120 notes`. The percent is worked out the way Clone Hero works it out, so it can read one below the exact share (53 of 100 notes reads `52%`). When you hit the solo's last note, the box shows `Perfect Solo!` for 1.5 seconds. Then it shows the solo bonus of 100 points per note, like `+12,000`, for 1.5 seconds, and then goes away. The path hits every note, so a solo always ends in `Perfect Solo!`. If a new solo starts while the box still shows the last one's result, the new solo takes over. The box works before a song is analyzed too.

**The step buttons move 10 ms.** `< 10 ms` and `10 ms >`, and the `,` (comma) and `.` (period) keys, move the playback position 10 ms. The buttons used to read `< 5 Ticks` and `5 Ticks >` and move 5 ticks. A tick is the chart's own smallest step of time. How long 5 ticks last depends on the tempo, so the old step changed with it. A step still pauses playback.

**The Star Power meter shows on every song.** The Star Power (SP) meter at the right edge of the Preview, and the drain box beside it, used to disappear on a chart with no Star Power phrases. The drain box shows how long one bar of SP lasts. Both now show on every song. On a chart with no Star Power the meter stays empty and reads `0.0/4` when the SP cap setting is 4, and the drain box reads `SP drain (if activated)`.
