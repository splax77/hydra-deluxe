Hydra Deluxe 2.9.4 fixes how drum lanes look in the Preview when Note Shuffle is on.

**Your saved results carry over.** Nothing reads `Stale` after the update, and nothing is analyzed again. Lanes only change what the Preview draws, not any score or path.

**A drum lane follows the shuffled notes.** A drum lane is the coloured strip the highway lights under a drum roll or trill. With Note Shuffle on, the lane used to stay on the pad the chart wrote, even after the shuffle had moved the notes under it to other pads. A lane now takes its colours from the shuffled notes.

**With Note Shuffle on, a lane lights every pad its notes land on.** Note Shuffle can turn a one-pad roll into notes that move between two or more pads. The lane now lights each of those pads, up to all four. Clone Hero still lights only the pad or pads it picks from the first notes under the lane, so here the Preview looks different from the game on purpose. With Note Shuffle off, lanes light the same pads as in Clone Hero, as before.
