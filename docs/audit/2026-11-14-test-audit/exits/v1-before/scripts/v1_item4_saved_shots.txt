# Run: hydra_uitest.exe --script <this file> --shots <folder for the pictures>
# The shading is a filled rectangle on the timeline's "saved" row, drawn with AddRectFilled (src/ui/preview_timeline.cpp:875), so it has no text.
# What prints: the activation bands' rects and the saved row's label rect (dump lines), the "left over" line of the Paths tab, and a picture
# of the timeline widget "##timeline". The picture is the evidence for the shading; the band rects say where the last activation ends.

click Scan library
