## SP-15

- Unknown was: whether the game's Star Power drain follows the time signature, given that the tick formula at `0x20D1D20` reads two segment fields, +0x30 and +0x40, whose meaning was not read.
- Looked at: new listings `code/6142/sp_units_from_time_20D1D20.asm` (lines 1-10), `code/6142/sp_segment_rates_20D2930.asm` (lines 45-82), `code/6142/sp_segments_build_20D3F30.asm` (lines 51-262) and `code/6142/sp_time_from_units_20D4A70.asm` (lines 1-7), each with its `.md`; the existing `code/6142/sp_end_time_20F37E0.asm` lines 22-83; `C:\Users\Patrick\Downloads\Hydra\hydra-data\ch-dumps\6142\dump.cs` lines 533997-534022 (class #8344), 534464-534480 (class #8349), 534609-534618 (class #8353) and 543384-543385 (the chart's +0xE0 and +0xE8 lists); Hydra's `src/core/timing.cpp` lines 113-149 and 311-338.
- Found: The game builds one timing segment per tempo or time signature change (`0x20D3F30`). It takes the tempo from the chart's +0xE0 list (class #8353, whose maker defaults to 120, so a tempo in beats per minute) and the two numbers from the +0xE8 list (class #8344, whose maker defaults to 4 and 4, so a time signature's top and bottom numbers). It stores the tempo at segment +0x30 (builder line 243) and the top and bottom numbers at +0x24 and +0x28 (lines 245-246). Then `0x20D2930` sets segment +0x40 to (4 / bottom) x top x 8 / 30 (lines 57-68), which is the measure's length in quarter notes times 8 / 30. The formula at `0x20D1D20` returns time x scale x tempo / (60 x +0x40) (lines 4-8). So one measure of any time signature is 30 / 8 = 3.75 x scale units: in 4/4 a measure is 4 beats, in 3/4 it is 3 beats, and both count 3.75 x scale. The full bar of 30 x scale (SP-11) is therefore 8 measures in any time signature. The segment's +0x38 is a running sum of these units across all earlier segments, each at its own rate (`0x20D2930` lines 69-79), and the end-time function walks the segments by +0x38 and turns units back into time with `0x20D4A70` (`sp_end_time_20F37E0.asm` lines 66-83). So the count runs without a break across a change, and a part measure before a change counts as its share of the old measure. The constants 4.0, 8.0 and 30.0 the 2026-10-12 session saw at `0x20D2930` are the ones on lines 45-47. One side finding: page 5 and `code/6142/sp_current_tick_20F3750.md` call +0x38 the segment's "start tick" and the result of `0x20F3750` a tick. They are Star Power units, 3.75 x scale per measure, which in 4/4 is 0.9375 x scale per beat, not chart ticks.
- Proposed label: Proven in the game's code. The four listings show the time signature's two numbers set the drain rate per beat.
- Hydra: agrees in rule, as far as read. Hydra counts measures continuously over a ticks-per-measure map (`MeasureCount`, used by `SongTiming::plusmeasure` in `src/core/timing.cpp` line 320), which is D231's rule, and a part measure counts as its share. Where Hydra fills that map from the time signatures was not traced in this round, so whether it uses top x 4 / bottom quarter notes exactly (and how it rounds to whole ticks) is not checked here.
