## IN-29

- Unknown was: whether `GlobalVariables.currentSongEntry` (+0x28) holds the song being played; no writer had been read.
- Looked at: `dump.cs` lines 55801 to 55867 (the class's fields). The field-write scan for +0x28 over every named method that loads GlobalVariables' type info or belongs to GlobalVariables (`W9a/globals_writes_28_7C.txt`). It found 16 stores: `GlobalVariables` 0x235AD0, two `SongSelect` methods (0x891D0 and 0x8D260, two stores each), five `EndOfSong` methods, five `PauseMenu` methods and one method of class `ʼʸʺʳʽʿʽʾʽˀʼ` (0x26A130). New listing `globals_set_song_235AD0.asm`. Callers of 0x235AD0, direct callers only (byte scan): `W9a/callers_235AD0.txt`. I also read the first `SongSelect` method (0x891D0) around its two stores, without keeping a listing.
- Found: the method that loads a song for play stores its SongEntry argument in `currentSongEntry` before anything else it does with the song (`globals_set_song_235AD0.asm` line 47, with no test in front of it; `this` is `rsi` from line 18, the song `rbx` from line 17). The song's chart is built after that (the chart builder `0x20D2EA0` at line 110), and a failure logs "Failed to load song object for" (lines 173 to 195). Its callers are `SongOptions` method 0x297240 and a coroutine `MoveNext` at 0x3BD710; neither was read, so which menu route reaches it is not shown here. In `SongSelect` 0x891D0, which first sets `isPlayingSetlist` (+0x74) to 1 at 0x89348, one store copies `selectedSong` (+0x20) into `currentSongEntry` when `selectedSong` is null (0x89367 to 0x89377), and the other stores an item read from a `List<SongEntry>` (0x8948D to 0x894AB). The `EndOfSong`, `PauseMenu` and 0x26A130 stores were not read, so whether something changes the field while a song plays is not settled.
- Proposed label: Proven in the game's code for a narrower rule: "The song-load method `0x235AD0` sets `currentSongEntry` to the song it loads, before it builds the chart." Whether the field still holds that song for the whole song stays Unknown, with this Evidence: the five `PauseMenu` stores and the five `EndOfSong` stores are where to read next, and the menu route to `0x235AD0` (through `SongOptions` 0x297240) was not read.
- Hydra: no owner.

## IN-39

- Unknown was: who writes Bot (2) into `GlobalVariables.scoreDisabled` (+0x7C) in 1.1.
- Looked at: `dump.cs` lines 39726 to 39733 (enum #727) and 55827 (the field). The field-write scan for +0x7C (`W9a/globals_writes_28_7C.txt`), which found seven stores. The existing listing `gm_load_coroutine_D8D80.asm` lines 531 to 560. New listings `globals_any_bot_235A40.asm`, `end_of_song_start_15DC20.asm` (lines 721 to 743 read) and `gm_score_disabled_4_B5F90.asm` (lines 126 to 196 read). The two tests `0x337540` and `0x13F8B0` were read with chdis and not kept (their note is in `globals_any_bot_235A40.md`).
- Found: two methods write Bot, and both write it only when `GlobalVariables` method `0x235A40` returns true. That method returns true when some player in `playerList` passes `0x337540` (it has a Rewired player at +0x18, or the remote byte +0xB1 of its +0x10 is set) and the "is_bot" test `0x13F8B0` (`globals_any_bot_235A40.asm` lines 19 to 32). The first writer is the GameManager load coroutine: the call at `gm_load_coroutine_D8D80.asm` line 547, the false branch at line 549 and the store of 2 at line 560. The second is `EndOfSong.Start`: the call at `end_of_song_start_15DC20.asm` line 729 and the store of 2 at line 742. The other five stores the scan found are 0 and 1 in `BasePlayer` 0x209C70 (at 0x209E5E and 0x209E90), 3 in `GameManager` 0xB9680 (at 0xB9A13), a register value in `EndOfSong` 0x161C70 (at 0x162A55), and 4 in the GameManager pause method 0xB5F90. Only the last of those was read: when scores are not already off and practice is not on, and fewer than 1185.0 units pass between the two doubles at GameManager +0x70 and +0x118 while `totalPauses` is above 3, it writes 4 (`gm_score_disabled_4_B5F90.asm` lines 126 to 196). The enum names no 4.
- Proposed label: Proven in the game's code: "The GameManager load coroutine and `EndOfSong.Start` write Bot when `GlobalVariables` method `0x235A40` finds a player with is_bot on." The block's Rule line should also say that the game writes a fifth value, 4, from the pause method, which the enum does not name.
- Hydra: no owner.

## IN-91

- Unknown was: whether the `PlayerSelectionMenu` method at RVA 0x32F590 runs once a frame, as the comment in `tools/ch_mcp/agent/menu.ts` (lines 49 to 54) says.
- Looked at: new listing `player_menu_on_ready_page_32F590.asm`. Callers, direct callers only (byte scan): `W9a/callers_32F590.txt`. New listing `player_canvas_update_D0E20.asm`, whose note gives every line range. `dump.cs` lines 25710 to 25722 (the canvas class and its fields), 73000 and 73001 (the method), and 72794 to 72803 (the page enum).
- Found: the method is not a per-frame method of the screen. It is a three-instruction test, "is this screen on its Ready page", that returns true when the page field +0x120 is 4 (`player_menu_on_ready_page_32F590.asm` lines 1 to 3). It has six direct callers: `PlayerSelectionCanvas.Update` at 0xD0EFF, three unnamed `PlayerSelectionCanvas` methods, and two `PlayerProfileMenu` methods. Each frame, while the canvas's bool at +0x48 is false and its component is enabled, `Update` calls it once on every player screen whose GameObject is active (`player_canvas_update_D0E20.asm` lines 13, 18, 25 to 58 and 97 to 99). So with one active screen it runs once a frame; with two, twice a frame. When every active screen is ready, `Update` fades to `Gameplay` and switches itself off (lines 100 to 105 and 130 to 162). The other five callers can run it more often; they were not read.
- Proposed label: Proven in the game's code, for a reworded rule: "0x32F590 tests whether the player screen is on its Ready page. `PlayerSelectionCanvas.Update` calls it once a frame on each active player screen, until every active screen is ready or the canvas's +0x48 bool is set. Five other methods call it too." The tools' comment is right for one player, but it calls the method the screen's per-frame method, and it is the canvas's per-frame call of a getter.
- Hydra: no owner. The tools use it as `PLAYER_SCREEN_FRAME_RVA` in `tools/ch_mcp/agent/menu.ts` line 54. This is not a Hydra split. It is a note for the tools: a watch on 0x32F590 counts one hit per active screen per frame, plus any from the five other callers.
