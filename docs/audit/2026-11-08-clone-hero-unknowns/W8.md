- Found: The game counts stars before it adds the solo bonus, and before it adds the clean-play bonus too. All four callers do the same three steps. First they add up each player's stats: the player's `+0x14` into the band's `+0x18`, `+0x10` into `+0x10`, `+0x18` into `+0x14`, and `+0x1C` into `+0x1C` (`band_stars_210E2A0.asm` lines 111 to 118). Then they call the star counter with the band's `+0x18` as the score and `+0x10` as the star base, and keep the count at `+0x20` (lines 171 to 175). Only after that do they add `+0x14` and then `+0x1C` into the score at `+0x18` (lines 176 to 181). The other three listings show the same lines (their `.md` files give the numbers). Which player field is which comes from the stats file's own writer. `stats_fill_112490.asm` lines 115 and 116 copy the player's `+0x18` into the field `score_submit.asm` line 262 writes as `solo_bonus_total`, and lines 127 and 128 copy `+0x1C` into the field line 272 writes as `clean_play_bonus`. The file's `score` (lines 107 to 110, written at `score_submit.asm` line 252) is the getter `0x2130490`, which is `+0x1C` plus `+0x18` plus `+0x14` (`player_total_score_2130490.asm`, the whole method). So the score the stars are counted on is the file's `score` less `solo_bonus_total` less `clean_play_bonus`. The dump agrees: the counter's first parameter carries the same scrambled name as the player's `+0x14` field, and its second the same name as the band's `+0x10` field (`dump.cs` lines 534567, 545702 and 534507). Where the count is read from `+0x20` and shown was not traced. One GameManager method (`0xBC750`, call at `0xBC835`) calls `band_stars_210E2A0`; the other three have no direct caller. The two other star counts the game keeps also leave the solo bonus out. The engine counts its own stars on its score `+0x94` (`engine_stats_20F3DC0.asm` lines 199 to 207), and that same `+0x94` is what becomes the player's `+0x14`, while the solo bonus is a separate engine field, `+0xA0`, that becomes the player's `+0x18` (lines 219, 220, 234 and 235). The HUD star bar during play (SC-30) is fed ScoreManager's `+0x40`, which is the sum of the engines' `+0x94`; the solo bonus `+0xA0` is summed into a separate field, `+0x44` (`score_manager_sum_1285A0.asm` lines 30 to 34, passed on at `score_manager_update_1286B0.asm` lines 91 to 93).
- Proposed label: Proven in the game's code. The four listings show the count taken before both bonuses are added, and the stats writer ties the two added fields to `solo_bonus_total` and `clean_play_bonus` by their JSON names.
- Hydra: agrees. `src/core/stars.h` line 51 (`score_without_solo`) and line 59 (`path_stars`) count stars on the total minus the solo bonus, and `docs/UserGuide.md` line 382 says the same to players. The game also leaves out the clean-play bonus. Hydra has no clean-play bonus, and `clean_play_bonus` is 0 in every saved file (`docs/clone-hero/06-scoring.md`, SC-37's table), so the two scores are the same on every run seen.

## SC-09

- Unknown was: what the fourth running total at engine `+0xD0` means, and what the two note flags the score adder tests (bits 0x20 and 0x10 of note `+0x84`) mean.
- Looked at: `docs/clone-hero/code/6142/add_note_score_20F6EB0.asm` lines 42 to 80; `docs/audit/note-shuffle/scout-flag/note_getters.txt` lines 3 and 25; `docs/audit/drum-lanes/play/f20f63f0_base_hit.asm` lines 78 to 119 (the base hit routine `0x20F63F0`); `docs/clone-hero/code/6142/note_hit_dynamics_20DE770.md` lines 13 and 14; the new listing `engine_stats_20F3DC0.asm` lines 279 to 282; `stats_fill_112490.asm` lines 125 and 126; `score_submit.asm` lines 266 to 270. Byte scans of `GameAssembly.dll`: for the two setters' mask constants (`mov r9d, 0xFFEF` and `0xFFDF`) and for `or ax, 0x10` and `or ax, 0x20`; for direct calls to the setters and getters; and for code that loads the engine's `+0xD0` and stores it at `+0x3C`.
- Found: The total at `+0xD0` is the stats file's `squeezed_score`, and the two flags mark squeezed notes. The flags are set in one place only. The setter of bit 0x10 is `0x214B6E0` and of bit 0x20 is `0x214B710` (the mask scan found each mask once, in those two methods), and the only direct calls to them are in the base hit routine `0x20F63F0`. There, bit 0x10 is set on a note hit while Star Power is on when it is scheduled at or before the activation, or after the end (`f20f63f0_base_hit.asm` lines 78 to 103, with the counter `+0xE8`, SP-36 on page 05). Bit 0x20 is set when Star Power is off and the note lies within the half-window `+0x10` of the last end `+0x128` (lines 104 to 119, with the counter `+0xEC`). The drum hit handler calls the base hit before it scores the note (`note_hit_dynamics_20DE770.md` lines 13 and 14), so the flags are in place when the adder reads them. The adder then adds half the multiplier times the points to `+0xD0` for a bit-0x10 note in Star Power (`add_note_score_20F6EB0.asm` lines 62 to 80), which is the Star Power share that note earned, and takes (multiplier minus 1) times the points away for a bit-0x20 note outside it (lines 45 to 54). The engine's stats method copies `+0xD0` to the player's `+0x3C` (`engine_stats_20F3DC0.asm` lines 281 and 282, the only such copy the scan found), the stats filler copies `+0x3C` to the record's `+0x50` (`stats_fill_112490.asm` lines 125 and 126), and the writer writes `+0x50` as `squeezed_score` (`score_submit.asm` lines 266 to 270). Two smaller hits of the `or ax` scan, `or ax, 0x10` in `0x20F0520` and `or ax, 0x20` in `0x20DE070`, were not opened; they may set the same word on another route, or another field. The mask scan found no other setter of either bit.
- Proposed label: Proven in the game's code for what writes and reads `+0xD0` and the two flags, and that `+0xD0` is `squeezed_score`. Calling it "the points squeezing won or lost" is my reading of the arithmetic, not a name in the code. The minus side's amount, (multiplier minus 1) times points, is what the code does; why that amount was chosen is not shown.
- Hydra: no owner. A case-blind grep of `src/` for `squeezed_score`, `squeezed points`, `squeezed_points` and `squeeze_points` finds nothing; Hydra keeps no running total of squeezed points. (`squeezed` alone appears 131 times in 21 files, for Hydra's own squeeze rating, which is not this total.) Hydra's own squeeze tests are `within_squeeze_window` (src/core/model.h line 123) and `sqout_chords`, which SP-36 already compares with the base hit's tests.

- Found: The cutoff rounds up, on both routes. The helper tests a stored CPU-feature value and picks a route (`ceil_helper_3031130.asm` lines 1 to 3). The fast route is one `roundsd xmm0, xmm0, 0xa` (`ceil_sse41_304BA10.asm` line 1), and mode 0xA rounds toward plus infinity. The software route, which the page left unread, also returns the ceiling (`ceil_soft_3031150.asm`). It cuts the fraction bits off (lines 30 to 37) and adds 1.0 when the number is positive and the cut changed it (lines 38 to 42). Numbers between 0 and 1 give 1.0 (lines 19 to 29), and whole numbers of 2 to the 52 or more come back as they are (lines 12 to 18). The helper is `Math.Ceiling` by its address: `System.Math.Ceiling` at `0x1B518E0` is a single jump to `0x3031130` (`math_ceiling_1B518E0.asm` line 1; `dump.cs` line 90193), and the star counter sets up `System.Math`'s class just before the call (`star_count_210DD00.asm` lines 39 to 45). The steps around it are as SC-25 says: an int times a float slot in 32-bit floats (lines 35 to 37), widened to a double (line 46), rounded up (line 47), then cut to a 32-bit int (line 48, `cvttsd2si ecx`).
- Proposed label: Proven in the game's code. Both routes are listed whole and both compute the ceiling.
