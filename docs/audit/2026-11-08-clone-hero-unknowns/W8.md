## SC-29

- Unknown was: whether the game's star count compares the cutoffs with the score without the solo bonus, as Hydra does; the four other callers of the star counter `0x210DD00` had not been opened.
- Looked at: new listings `docs/clone-hero/code/6142/band_stars_210E2A0.asm`, `band_stars_210F140.asm`, `band_stars_21104F0.asm` and `band_stars_2110920.asm` (the four callers, whole), `stats_fill_112490.asm` (the method that copies a player's stats into the stats file's record) and `player_total_score_2130490.asm` (the player's total-score getter), each with its `.md`. Also the committed `star_count_210DD00.asm`, `score_submit.asm` lines 252 to 275, and `C:\Users\Patrick\Downloads\Hydra\hydra-data\ch-dumps\6142\dump.cs` lines 534504 to 534570 (class #8351 and the counter's signature) and 545698 to 545728 and 545801 (class #8548). A byte scan of `GameAssembly.dll` for calls to `0x210DD00` found exactly the four callers, and no other direct call.
- Found: The game counts stars before it adds the solo bonus, and before it adds the clean-play bonus too. All four callers do the same three steps. First they add up each player's stats: the player's `+0x14` into the band's `+0x18`, `+0x10` into `+0x10`, `+0x18` into `+0x14`, and `+0x1C` into `+0x1C` (`band_stars_210E2A0.asm` lines 111 to 118). Then they call the star counter with the band's `+0x18` as the score and `+0x10` as the star base, and keep the count at `+0x20` (lines 171 to 175). Only after that do they add `+0x14` and then `+0x1C` into the score at `+0x18` (lines 176 to 181). The other three listings show the same lines (their `.md` files give the numbers). Which player field is which comes from the stats file's own writer. `stats_fill_112490.asm` lines 115 and 116 copy the player's `+0x18` into the field `score_submit.asm` line 262 writes as `solo_bonus_total`, and lines 127 and 128 copy `+0x1C` into the field line 272 writes as `clean_play_bonus`. The file's `score` (lines 107 to 110, written at `score_submit.asm` line 252) is the getter `0x2130490`, which is `+0x1C` plus `+0x18` plus `+0x14` (`player_total_score_2130490.asm`, the whole method). So the score the stars are counted on is the file's `score` less `solo_bonus_total` less `clean_play_bonus`. The dump agrees: the counter's first parameter carries the same scrambled name as the player's `+0x14` field, and its second the same name as the band's `+0x10` field (`dump.cs` lines 534567, 545702 and 534507). Where the count is read from `+0x20` and shown was not traced. One GameManager method (`0xBC750`, call at `0xBC835`) calls `band_stars_210E2A0`; the other three have no direct caller. One thread is left open: the HUD star bar during play (SC-30) is fed a different score, ScoreManager's `+0x40`. It is the sum of each engine's `+0x94` (ScoreManager method `0x1285A0`, instructions at `0x128619` and `0x128622`, passed at `0x128831` to `0x128837`). Whether the solo bonus goes into `+0x94` was not read, so whether the bar can light a star mid-song that the final count does not give is not known.
- Proposed label: Proven in the game's code. The four listings show the count taken before both bonuses are added, and the stats writer ties the two added fields to `solo_bonus_total` and `clean_play_bonus` by their JSON names.
