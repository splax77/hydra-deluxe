- **Splits:** None. The sentence and the code answer the same question, "how long before the fill must SP be ready". Ch11: sentence "4 beats before the fill starts" vs `fill_start_tick(...) - kCh11FillLeadBeats * timing.tick_resolution()` with `kCh11FillLeadBeats = 4` (src/search/graph.h:71, src/search/graph.cpp:68-69). The deadline is `fst - preroll`, so it sits about one fill-length before the fill start. The sentence is a plain-words rounding of that; it leaves out the 250 ms floor and 10 s ceiling, which only bite for a fill shorter than 0.25 s or longer than 10 s.
- **How it left:** a code-only call, then fixed. Closure's words: "M38 (the "4 beats" sentence): the number comes from the constant, sentence unchanged." (docs/audit/2026-10-11-concept-audit/questions.md:219, "Code-only findings" list); V4.md:174 "The Ch10 sentence carries no number on purpose." is the auditor's word, not a ruling. Fixed in 57c5da7b "M38: one constant for Clone Hero 1.1's fill lead in beats".
- **User's words:** the code-only list opens "These proceed on your yes to the fix wave. Veto by id." (questions.md:217); the user's reply to the 21 questions was "go with recommended", recorded in D114: "The user read the 21 questions ... and replied "go with recommended"." (docs/audit/2026-10-03-fix-decisions.md:553). Nothing was asked about the Ch10 wording itself.
- **Code today:** the displayed text is `fill_rule_description` (src/search/graph.h:75-81): Ch11 "Clone Hero 1.1 sets the deadline 4 beats before the fill starts.", Ch10 "Clone Hero 1.0 set the deadline about one fill-length before the fill starts." Printed by the settings tooltip (src/ui/settings_bar.cpp:24-30) and the fill report footer. The User Guide (docs/UserGuide.md:156) says "Clone Hero 1.1 wants it ready 4 beats before the fill. 1.0 wanted it about one fill-length before, so short fills were looser and long fills stricter." That matches the code: with lead L = fill length and the 1.1 lead = 4 beats, a fill shorter than 4 beats gets a later (looser) 1.0 deadline and a longer one an earlier (stricter) one, within the clamp. The game's own rule agrees: docs/clone-hero/04-fills.md FL-19/FL-20 (1.1, four quarter notes in every meter) and FL-42 (1.0, own length held between 0.25 s and 10 s). By reading. Only other "4 beats" in src, tools and public pages: CONTEXT.md:609, which is internal.
- **Verdict:** PASS
- **Why:** The displayed sentence and the User Guide describe the code's real rule in plain words; "about" covers the res/16 pad, and the clamp only matters for fills under 0.25 s or over 10 s. The 4 now comes from one constant. No split.
