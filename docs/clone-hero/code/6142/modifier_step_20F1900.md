# modifier_step_20F1900.asm
The modifier step the note builder calls for modifier bit 0x20 (NL-68). It mirrors each note's +0x80 lane word, by instrument: line 9 takes the instrument from its second argument and lines 26 to 31 jump through a 15-entry table at 0x20F1E28 (raw bytes at lines 298 to 309). Read from the DLL, entries 6 and 9 (the drum instruments of NL-43) go to 0x20F1BD7 (line 160); entries 4, 5, 10, 11 and 14 go to 0x20F1984 (line 32); entries 0 to 3 and 7 go to 0x20F1AB6 (line 98); entries 8, 12 and 13 return. On drums, lines 182 to 196 move red to green tom, yellow tom to blue tom, blue tom to yellow tom and green tom to red, and leave the kick. Lines 231 to 259 then move the cymbals using the chord's pattern from 0x214BCD0 minus the note's own bits: a yellow cymbal becomes the green cymbal, or the blue cymbal when the chord has a green tom; a blue cymbal stays, or becomes the yellow cymbal when the chord has a blue tom; a green cymbal becomes the yellow cymbal, or the blue cymbal when the chord has a yellow tom. Page 2's NL-69 cites this listing.
