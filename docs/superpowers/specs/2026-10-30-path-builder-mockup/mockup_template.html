About this mockup

Every score is Hydra's engine output for this chart under your settings (Expert Pro Drums, 2x kick, SP cap 4, 10 ms Path limit), taken with hydra_replay. The mockup holds engine-priced paths instead of running the search live. Squeeze timings are the engine's own figures, printed the way Hydra prints "needs X ms". The Star Power line is a sketch drawn from the phrases you save. Left out: early-fill timing, Copy and Paste, and Save and Open.
