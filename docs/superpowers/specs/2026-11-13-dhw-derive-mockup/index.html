Mockup. The Dynamic Hit Window row on seven real activations, as the plan would draw it.
Click an activation to open or close it. "Expand all" opens every one in its panel. Each opened activation shows its squeeze sentence (today's text, unchanged), the new row under it, and a grey line with the row Hydra 3.0.0 draws today. How to read the new row. It starts with Helps: when the timing needs hitting: these are the presses that make it. It starts with Hurts: when the timing needs none: these are the presses that would undo it. The notes are in song order, joined by then. The tag says what the game's windows allow. Each figure is one side of that note's window. frontend is the side before the note (a press ahead of it). backend is the side after the note (a press behind it). These two words replace "early" and "late" on the row, by the user's ruling of 2026-11-13. The sentence above the row still says "early" and "late"; whether it changes too is question 3 of the plan.
