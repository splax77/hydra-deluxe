# The second Path Builder mockup: what the user approved (2026-10-31)

Session 8bb2c4a3. The mockup answers the six problems from the second real-app check (`docs/handoffs/2026-10-30-path-builder-real-app-check-2-handoff.md`). The spec is `mockup_template.html` in this folder; `path-builder-mockup-2.html` is the same page with its data inside, so it opens on its own.

The user chose the song: asked "The new mockup needs a busy song so the crowding problem shows. Which song should it use?", the user answered "The Reaper (Recommended)". The data is Hydra's engine output for "(Don't Fear) The Reaper (Rh)", Expert Pro Drums, 2x kick, SP cap 4, 10 ms Path limit; `build_data.py` says how to rebuild it.

The mockup carried four open questions, each with the recommendation selected by default and the other option one click away in its purple bar. After clicking through, asked "After clicking through, do the four defaults hold?", the user answered "Yes, all four defaults". So:

1. **How costs show.** Colours plus a "Where to activate next" list. On the timeline each fill you can activate at is tinted by its cost tier (green: the best, costs nothing; yellow: under 1% of the best score; red: more), and only the best one has a word under it ("best"). No number sits under any fill. Beside the Preview, a list names every fill you can activate at next, cheapest first: the fill, its clock, phrases saved, where Hydra would end it and any squeeze, and the cost ("best" or "−2,060"). Hovering a row lights its fill on the timeline and moves the Preview there; clicking it activates there. The fill's hover tooltip keeps the exact numbers. Rejected: colours only, with no list.
2. **Fills before your last activation.** A locked look: a small grey dot with its own legend entry ("Before your last activation"). A click on one says "<fill> is before your last activation. You build left to right; Undo or Remove goes back a step." The hollow ring now means only "the game won't let you activate here". The left-to-right ruling (Q1 of the 2026-10-30 plan) stands. Rejected: making them clickable to re-plan from there.
3. **How the saved row shows "enough".** The caption moves into the row's label, left: "Star Power saved", then a dashed sample and "2 phrases: needed to activate", then a teal swatch and "enough to activate". After the last activation, the stretches where the saved line is at or above the dashed line are shaded in teal. Nothing is written at the row's right end. Rejected: the caption alone, with no shading.
4. **The layout.** Top to bottom: title and mode with the three scores (Your score, Best you can still reach, Best possible) and Undo, Start over, Finish the rest for me; one intro line; the guide line; the timeline panel (legend, axis, sections, phrases, saved, Hydra's best when ticked, your activations, fills). Under it, the Preview panel takes the width, and a column about 27 rem wide holds "Where to activate next" above "Your activations". The Preview panel is the whole of Hydra's Preview: the "< Act" and "Act >" buttons (jumping between the player's activations), -5s, < 10 ms, Play, 10 ms >, +5s, Vol, the scrub bar with a gold mark per activation and the clock, the highway with its time box, score box, SP gauge, drain box and next-activation box, and the key hints under it. The steps are compact rows ("m32 → m36", phrases, cost); the selected one opens to the full sentence, end choices on the last one, and Remove.

Facts the mockup checked against the engine (all from `hydra_replay` on this chart): the meter rule it draws matched the engine's bars at all 13,809 activations in its data; the player's path from the check (m32, m48, m87, m153) gave the real app's costs (480, 2,060, 28,600, 12,620) and score (864,325); the score box's running total matched `hydra_replay score` at 2:28.655 (359,625).

Every size in the mockup is in rem, so the whole screen follows one font size. The real builder's timeline text must follow the user's font size like the rest of Hydra, not fixed pixels (the PV-4 bug in the handoff).
