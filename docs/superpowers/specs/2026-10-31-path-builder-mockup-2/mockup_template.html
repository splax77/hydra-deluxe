About this mockup

The song is the one from the real-app check: (Don't Fear) The Reaper (Rh), Expert Pro Drums with 2x kick, SP cap 4, 10 ms Path limit. Every score, cost and end choice is Hydra's engine output (hydra_replay) for this chart. The mockup holds engine-priced path prefixes instead of running the search live. The running score in the Preview's score box is the no-Star-Power score plus each window's Star Power bonus, and each window's bonus is the engine's own figure.

The highway is a canvas sketch of Hydra's Preview, with its real controls, scrub bar and overlay boxes worded as Hydra words them. There is no audio. In the real builder this whole panel is Hydra's Preview code, shared, not a copy.

All sizes here follow one font size. In the real builder the timeline's text follows your Hydra font size like everything else.

The purple bar at the top is not part of the screen. It switches between the options for the open questions. Click anywhere to close this.
