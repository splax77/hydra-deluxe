About this mockup

The song is the long one from the real-app check: Divine Inner Tension (Album) by Hail The Sun, 42 minutes, Expert Pro Drums with 2x kick, SP cap 4, 10 ms Path limit. Every score, cost and end choice is Hydra's engine output (hydra_replay) for this chart. The mockup holds engine-priced path prefixes instead of running the search live. To keep that set small enough to build, it holds only the paths within 20,000 points of the best, so a fill no such path activates at shows grey ("no engine numbers").

What changed from the second mockup: hovering a fill shows it but never plays, and moving off jumps back and stays paused. The pointer picks the nearest fill anywhere in the Fills row. The fill's tooltip is a short card. The timeline shows a window of the song, with the whole song in the strip above it. The 3 minutes and 2.5 rem per measure are stand-ins for the mockup, not settled numbers. While playing, the window turns a page when the playhead passes three quarters of the way across. The running score in the Preview's score box is the no-Star-Power score plus each window's Star Power bonus, and each window's bonus is the engine's own figure.

The highway is a canvas sketch of Hydra's Preview, with its real controls, scrub bar and overlay boxes worded as Hydra words them. There is no audio. In the real builder this whole panel is Hydra's Preview code, shared, not a copy.

All sizes here follow one font size. In the real builder the timeline's text follows your Hydra font size like everything else.

The purple bar at the top is not part of the screen. It switches between the options for the open questions. Click anywhere to close this.
