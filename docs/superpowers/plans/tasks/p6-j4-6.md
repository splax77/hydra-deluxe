## What changes

**`tests/temp_util.h`**, new, beside `tests/corpus_util.h`, namespace `testtemp` (the pattern of `testsrb`, `testmidi`, `testbytes`). Two functions, header-only:

- `temp_path(tag, ext)`: `<temp>hydra_test_<tag>_<pid><ext>` as UTF-8. One `GetTempPathW`, one `wide_to_utf8`, the tag and extension appended as given. It creates nothing. This is the shape the four `hydra_test_` copies already use, so their names do not move.
- `temp_dir(tag)`: `<temp>hydra_test_<tag>_<pid>` as UTF-8, created if missing (`fs::create_directories` through `hydra::os_path`, as `test_long_paths.cpp` does). A caller that wants it empty calls `remove_all` first, as `test_utf8_paths.cpp` and the `DocumentsSandbox` do today.

The tag is UTF-8 and may hold non-ASCII (`test_utf8_paths.cpp` passes its "Zoë"); the helper never converts it. The header's comment says it owns the per-process scratch recipe and names `GetTempPathW` as the one call under `tests/`.

**The twelve callers.** Each copy becomes a call, keeping a word from its old name as the tag so a stray folder is still recognisable: `test_app_state.cpp` and `test_song_panel_state.cpp` keep their `temp_path(tag, ext)` wrappers only if they add something (they do not; delete them and call the owner). `test_sng.cpp` calls `temp_path("sng_" + name, "")`-style with the name in the tag. `test_winstr.cpp`'s `TempFile` takes a tag, calls `temp_path(tag, ext)` and converts once with `utf8_to_wide` for its `CreateFileW` (it gains the pid, which it lacked). `test_long_paths.cpp` calls `temp_dir(tag)` and converts the UTF-8 root back to wide to grow its deep path. `test_srb.cpp`'s hand `WideCharToMultiByte` goes with its copy. `test_preview_source.cpp`, `test_preview_load_progress.cpp`, `test_preview_controller.cpp` (both functions), `test_analysis.cpp`'s `scan_fixture_dir`, `test_report.cpp` (both sites) and `test_utf8_paths.cpp` call `temp_dir` or `temp_path`. Where a copy also built the wide form for a Win32 call, convert the owner's UTF-8 once at that call.

**274.** `rel_of` in `test_analysis.cpp` goes; its two callers (around lines 97 and 108) call `hydra::relative_slash_path`. Delete J2-1's known-copy row for it.
