## The plain version

The first rebuild put the right facts on screen but the user could not read them, and it left most of Hydra's Preview out. The user's last words were "yeah no this is unreadable. we need a new approach." The second mockup fixes that in four ways, and the user approved all four defaults: costs show as colours on the fills plus a sorted "Where to activate next" list; fills before your last activation get a small locked dot instead of the ring; the saved row's caption moves to the left and the stretches where you have enough saved are shaded teal; and the whole of Hydra's Preview panel sits under the timeline with a narrow column beside it for the list and your activations.

So this plan does five builds and one check: SP-1, TL-2 and SC-3a run in parallel, then SC-3b and TS-4. One task moves the body of Hydra's Preview panel into shared code, so Hydra's tab and the builder draw the same thing. One task changes the timeline: text at the user's font size, tinted fills with only "best" written under one, the locked look, the caption and the shading. One task writes the builder's new words and rules (the refusal kinds, the list's rows and order, the compact step). One task lays the builder out around the shared panel, adds the list, makes the steps compact rows, and drops the fixed 600 px highway. Then the click-through test is brought up to date, the user sees it in the real app on The Reaper, the documents are written, one fresh reviewer reads the whole branch, and it merges.

## Engine truth, not screen rules

Nothing in this plan computes a game fact on the screen. Which fills can be activated is the engine's list (`trailing_skipped_fill_ticks`, EN-4). Why a fill can't is `fill_reason`. A cost is `candidate_costs`. A tier is `cost_tier`. Where Hydra would end an activation is the candidate's activation from `candidate_end`. How much is saved at a moment is the Preview's meter curve (`SpMeterCurve` segments, `sp_meter_bars_at`). The shading is a reading of that curve against the dashed activate line, in the timeline's pure layout, beside the saved line it already draws from the same segments: a stretch is "enough" where the saved line is at or above `activate_y`, which `timeline_layout` already sets from `kSpActivationBars`. That way `kSpActivationBars` is not compared a second time, which the single-owner row `tests/single_owner/rules/pathbuilder/activate-line.json` forbids outside `fill_reason` (its scope lists `src/ui/preview_timeline.cpp`). The locked look is the one builder-owned rule, left to right (Q1 of the first plan), and it is read from `fill_reason`'s facts, not re-derived.
