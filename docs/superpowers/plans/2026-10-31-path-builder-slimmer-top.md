# Path Builder: a slimmer top (2026-10-31)

Session 711858a7, task PBR5-PLAN. Branch `claude/pbr5-plan`, cut from `claude/pb-redesign`.

At the RA-7 real-app check the user opened The Reaper in the builder and said: "getting better but still way too much clutter in the interface, especially up top". Asked "Mock up a cleaner top area before changing any code?", they chose "Yes, mock it". They clicked through `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-31-path-builder-mockup-5/top-mockup.html` and answered "Looks right". `answers.md` beside it lists what the page changes, and `builder-top-before.webp` is the screen that prompted it.

This plan makes the real builder's top match that page. It changes no words the screen already has, moves two groups of words into hover text, and takes four things off the screen. One thing the page did not cover, the messages the coach line also carried, was question 1 below; the user answered it "Errors to top line (Rec.)" and T2b builds that answer.

## What the page shows, in one paragraph

Between the song line and the highway the builder today stacks: the song line (title, three scores, Undo, Start over, Finish the rest for me); a two-line intro paragraph; a coach line in a bordered box; a legend row of seven colour keys plus "Click anywhere on the timeline to move the highway there." and the "Show Hydra's best path" box; then the timeline's rows (axis, Section, Star Power phrases, Star Power saved with a two-line caption under its name, Hydra's best when ticked, Your activations, Fills) and the scroll row. The page keeps the song line and puts "Show Hydra's best path" at its right end beside the three buttons. The intro, the coach line, the legend row and the "Star Power phrases" row are gone. The stars sit along the top edge of the "Star Power saved" graph, which keeps its dashed 2-phrase line and its shading. The activation bands share one row with the fills; that row is labelled "Your activations" and each band starts at the fill it was made from. The legend's words become hover text: the "Star Power saved" label shows the phrases, activate-line and shading keys, and the "Your activations" label shows the six fill keys.

## What a player sees change

A player who opens a chart with Star Power sees these differences, and only these. The intro paragraph ("Build your own Star Power path, left to right. ...") is gone. The bordered coach line under it is gone, so its three steady sentences are gone too: the "Step 1. Click a glowing fill ..." sentence before the first activation, the "Your Star Power runs until m72. Save 2 more phrases, then activate again. ..." sentence between activations, and the "... so your path is finished. Your score is ..." sentence at the end. The one-shot messages that line also carried follow the user's answer to question 1 ("Errors to top line (Rec.)"). A click on a fill the player can't take shows nothing new, and the fill's hover card already says why. "Activation removed.", "Removed activation <N> and everything after it.", "Started over. ..." and "Hydra built the whole path ..." / "Hydra kept your 1 activation ..." are gone, since the steps panel and the score show the result. The engine's refusals ("This window makes one of your later windows impossible, ..." and its three siblings) and the pricing errors (why the path on screen has no ladder) show on the window's existing top refusal line, in its warning colour with its detail line. The legend row is gone, with its sentence "Click anywhere on the timeline to move the highway there." and its seventh key "You can activate here" (the page's hover text lists six keys, as `answers.md` says; the seventh was for a fill whose cost had not landed yet). "Show Hydra's best path" moves up to the right of "Finish the rest for me". The "Star Power phrases" row is gone; the same stars now sit along the top edge of the "Star Power saved" row, at the same moments as before. The two-line caption under "Star Power saved" ("2 phrases: needed to activate" with its dashed sample, "enough to activate" with its shaded swatch) leaves the label column and becomes the hover text of that label, with a star line "Star Power phrases" above them (question 2 asks whether the hover shows the drawn marks or the page's typed stand-ins). The "Fills" row and its label are gone; its dots, "best" words and hit boxes now sit in the "Your activations" row together with the bands, which keep their numbers and the selected outline. Hovering that row's label shows the six legend keys with their swatches: "Best place to activate", "Costs under 1% of the best score", "Costs more", "Your activation", "Can't activate here (hover to see why)", "Before your last activation". A click on a band still selects its step; a click elsewhere in the row still picks the nearest fill. The hover card on a fill, the list, the steps panel, the Preview and the scroll row do not change. Nothing changes for a chart with no Star Power beyond the intro no longer being drawn (it was not drawn for such a chart anyway).

## Questions for the user

1. **Where do the coach line's one-shot messages go?** Answered by the user. Asked "With the coach line gone, where should its after-a-click messages go?", they picked "Errors to top line (Rec.)", whose option text was "Engine refusals (2) and pricing errors (4) use the existing top refusal line. The question as asked: The page removes the coach line but says nothing about the messages it carried after an action. The code shows four groups.

   The first group is a refusal after a click on a fill the player can't take. The rule is `PbApp::click_fill` (`src/pathbuilder/pb_window.cpp` lines 789 to 798): it calls `fill_reason`, and a `BeforeLast` kind shows `locked_message(fill)`, any other refusal kind shows `cant_activate_message(fill, reason)`, both drawn red-edged in the coach box. `fill_reason` (`src/pathbuilder/pb_text.cpp` lines 83 to 110) has four refusal sentences. On Burnout (Green Day, `testdata/input`, the GUI tests' chart) with no activation, a click on the fill at m12 shows today, in the coach line: "Can't activate at m12: You have 1 phrase saved here. You need 2 to activate." (pinned in `tests/pb_ui/pbtest_coach.cpp` line 125). The same fill's hover card already reads, under its heading "m12 · <clock>": "You have 1 phrase saved here. You need 2 to activate." (the card adds the `fill_reason` sentence as its grey line, `pb_text.h` lines 189 to 200). On Burnout with one activation (the step row "m58 → m68 4 phrases −13,640"), a click on m12 shows: "m12 is before your last activation. You build left to right; Undo or Remove goes back a step." (`pbtest_coach.cpp` line 153), and the card reads "This is before your last activation. You build left to right; Undo or Remove goes back a step." (`tests/test_pb_text.cpp` line 419). The other two sentences follow the same shape: "Can't activate at m<N>: Star Power is already on here. Activation <k> runs until m<M>." and "Can't activate at m<N>: The game doesn't show this fill here."; the card shows each without the "Can't activate at m<N>: " head. No test pins a corpus example of those two; the plan does not invent one.

   The second group is the engine's refusal when a click, an end choice or "Finish the rest for me" cannot be realized: `DecisionList::message()` with its `detail()` line (`src/pathbuilder/pb_coach.h` lines 351 to 353), shown red-edged with the detail line under it (`pb_window.cpp` lines 550 and 581 to 582). Its sentences are `impossible_window_text` (`pb_coach.cpp` lines 54 to 66): "This window makes one of your later windows impossible, so the path stays as it was.", "Star Power can't be activated at this fill on your path: the meter isn't ready, or the fill doesn't appear.", "Star Power can't end where this window was pinned to end.", "This window can't end with the squeeze-out it was pinned to.", else `kSomethingWentWrong`; or `plain_error(e)` when the worker threw. No hover shows any of these. No test pins a corpus example.

   The third group is a confirmation after an action: "Activation removed." or "Removed activation <N> and everything after it." after Remove (`pb_window.cpp` line 1022), "Started over. Click a glowing fill to make your first activation." after Start over (line 273), and after Finish the rest for me either "Hydra built the whole path the best way. Undo takes it back." or "Hydra kept your 1 activation and added the best rest of the path. Undo takes it back." (Burnout, `pbtest_coach.cpp` line 105). Each is drawn plain, not red.

   The fourth group is `DecisionList::error()` with `error_detail()`: why the path on screen has no ladder (`pb_coach.h` lines 354 to 358), drawn in the warning colour inside the coach box (`pb_window.cpp` lines 583 to 590). Nothing else shows it.

   The screens that read these are: the builder's coach box (the only reader today), the fill's hover card (already shows group one's sentence, without the "Can't activate at m12: " head), and the window's top refusal line, which already shows a refused paste, Open path... or Save path... in the warning colour with a detail line and clears when the path changes (`pb_window.cpp` lines 378 to 385). A click can also come from the highway (`draw_preview`, lines 833 to 837), where no card is showing, and from a list row, which lists only fills after the last activation so it can never be refused by group one.

   Options:
   - A. Every one-shot message, all four groups, moves to the window's top refusal line (the existing mechanism for a refused paste), with the same clear-on-change rule.
   - B. Group one is dropped: the click does nothing and the fill's card, already under the pointer, says why. Groups two and four move to the top refusal line. Group three is dropped: the steps panel, the score and the Undo button already show what happened.
   - C. Keep a one-line box under the song line that is empty except while a message shows (the page has no such line; this would be a new screen element the user did not click through).

   Recommendation: B. It adds no new screen element, reuses the refusal line the window already has, and every word it keeps already exists. Its one loss: a click on an untakeable fill on the highway gets no feedback (the card shows only on the timeline). If that matters, A keeps the feedback at the cost of a line that appears after every refused click.

2. **The "Star Power saved" hover: drawn marks or typed stand-ins?** The page's hover is an HTML `title`, which can only hold text, so it types "★ Star Power phrases", "- - - 2 phrases: needed to activate" and "Shaded: enough to activate" (`top-mockup.html` line 546). The builder's label column today draws the dashed sample and the shaded swatch beside those words (`src/ui/preview_timeline.cpp` lines 154 to 184), and draws every star with `draw_star`, never as a text glyph. Options: the hover draws the star, the dashed sample and the swatch beside the words "Star Power phrases", "2 phrases: needed to activate" and "enough to activate" (the words the code has, the marks the column has now); or the hover types the page's stand-ins "- - - " and "Shaded: " as text, with the star drawn. Recommendation: the drawn marks, because the hover then matches what the row draws. Task T1 is written for the drawn marks and names this question. Answered by the user: asked "The Star Power saved label's hover: draw the real marks beside the words, or type them as the mockup did?", they picked "Draw the marks (Rec.)".
