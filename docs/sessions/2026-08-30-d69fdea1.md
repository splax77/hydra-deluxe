## Outcome
The session ended at 04:17 UTC with the report built and the dmleaderboards check done. The changes were left uncommitted.
The report is build-cpp/fillcompare/fill_compare.html. It covers all 18,494 drum charts in the library. 79 charts score higher under CH 1.1, 600 score higher under CH 1.0, and 17,815 are unchanged. The biggest gain is Night Verses at +28,900.
The Coma Machine test chart (Grinnz) gains 820 points under CH 1.1, from 1,067,105 to 1,067,925. Its path grows from 6 to 7 activations.
Of the 79 higher charts, 4 have a posted score from the user. Three of those posted scores equal the old CH 1.0 optimal.
The session added a hidden engine switch for the old rule, a hydra_batch --legacy-fills flag and a hydra_fillcompare tool, all in the hydra-test working tree. All 220 tests passed.
