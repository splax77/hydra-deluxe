Findings from the runs done before the stop, on a warm --redo of 7.1 s: about 1.9 s passes before the first chart starts (program start, folder listing, file checks, library table rewrite). Analysis takes 4.8 s, and the 8 workers are busy 93% of that time, waiting on the writer only 4%. The cap is the fixed 8-worker limit, and with 15 workers the single writer thread becomes the limit, mostly because of one progress line printed per chart. The earlier 10 s figure is out of date: the summary-only storage change from an earlier decision brought the run to about 7 s.
