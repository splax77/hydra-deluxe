## Outcome
The session ended with the proof published as an artifact, and the repository left unmodified. All temporary files stayed in the scratchpad.
The headline result: the unconditional floor is 5 points, reached only by a synthetic 9-note witness chart (the engine reports 695 against 690, matching the hand count). The floor is 20 points when no activation window can reach the first 29 notes, which holds for 18,539 of 18,552 parseable charts (from the library scan of 18,610 charts).
On real charts, the smallest gap observed is 10 points, in The Cars — Just What I Needed, from one pad covered at 3x. No real chart shows a 5-point gap. All 49,719 golden-corpus gaps are multiples of 20.
Three adversarial subagents checked the mutation sites, the witness and the empirical claims. Their fixes went into the artifact.
